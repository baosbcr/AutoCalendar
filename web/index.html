AutoCalendar

Turn an Outlook calendar export, or any planning sheet, into one .ics file that Outlook imports in one go.

Loading the converter…

Your file never leaves your computer. The converter runs inside this page. It also never invites anyone: invitees in the sheet are left out of the calendar file.
Advanced options

Update the Master too. The Master sheet on the course OneDrive is the source of truth, and edits made here do not reach it. Make the same changes in the Master. Do not replace the Master with the downloaded sheet: that sheet leaves out the Status and Day columns and the links in the Subject column.

    Then import it into Outlook

    1. In classic Outlook: File > Open & Export > Import/Export.
    2. Choose Import an iCalendar (.ics) or vCalendar file and pick the downloaded file.
    3. Click Import to add the events to your calendar, or Open as New to look them over in a separate calendar first.

    Use the menu rather than double-clicking the file: on some PCs a double-click opens the new Outlook, which gets stuck at a sign-in screen. How the sheet should look, and what you can edit: the guide.
